## Parity items we must NOT lose (17hats strengths to copy faithfully)

- 3-in-1 Quote → Contract → Invoice bundle in one client session (04).
- Choose One / Choose Any quote items with editable or locked quantities (04).
- Lead form → contact + project + tags + workflow, with branch-by-answer (02, 07).
- "Upon your review" approval option on any automated step (07).
- Document completion triggers gating the next workflow step (07).
- Relative dates in templates (valid-until, due date) (04, 05).
- Tips at checkout, save card, auto-pay per installment (05).
- Tokens in every template type, filtered by email type (06).
- Renameable document labels (Quote → Proposal) in Brand settings (01).
- Multi-brand support with brand switcher (01).
